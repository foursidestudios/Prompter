Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Prompter is a free teleprompter web app made by Fourside Studios LLC. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your scripts stay on your device, and we do not collect, store or sell anything about you.

Your scripts and settings

Scripts, settings and remote button assignments are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you are using. They are never sent to Fourside Studios or to any server we run. Clearing your browser data, or deleting Prompter from your home screen, removes them.

Importing from Google Docs

If you choose to import from Google Docs, Prompter asks Google for permission to open only the specific files you pick in Google's file picker (the drive.file permission). Prompter cannot see the rest of your Google Drive.

When you pick a document, your browser downloads its text directly from Google and saves it on your device as a script. That text is never sent to Fourside Studios. The access Google grants is short-lived and is not stored by us. You can remove Prompter's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access.

Prompter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Prompter does not use analytics, advertising, cookies for tracking, or any third-party tracking scripts.

Update checks

Prompter checks a small file on this website to see whether a newer version is available. That request contains no personal information.

Children

Prompter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
